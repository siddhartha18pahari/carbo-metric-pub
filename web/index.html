Spectral Gaussian splatting

The fitted map becomes a field of 3-D Gaussians in (x, y, Raman shift). Each ellipsoid sits over the part of a particle it describes, at the height of the band it carries. Fitted on the baseline-corrected cube, on the particles only, with every 5th channel held out to measure how well it generalises.

Detection and quality
height ≥standard errors
G ≥× noise
A band is detected when its fitted height is at least this many standard errors above zero. The standard error comes from the particle's own fit (its residual through the fit's covariance), so a broad weak band such as D3 is judged against every channel under it, not against the noise of one channel. Quantified: G at least 10 × the residual noise, inside its fit bounds (a band stuck on a bound is fitting noise), passing the Constraints of the Carbon fit tab. Everything below uses the quantified particles.
