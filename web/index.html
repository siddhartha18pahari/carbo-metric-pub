Carbon Raman maps, fitted pixel by pixel.

Open a LabSpec 6 map (.l6m) or a text export from any instrument (.txt, .csv: a LabSpec matrix, an x / y / shift / intensity table, or a single spectrum). Q-Carbon's own result exports open too, as maps and statistics of their fitted values. Carbo Metrics fits every spectrum with an editable five-band carbon model, maps D, G and their ratio, shows the white-light image underneath when there is one, and can turn the whole hyperspectral cube into a 3-D Gaussian splat field.

Spectral Gaussian splatting

The fitted map becomes a field of 3-D Gaussians in (x, y, Raman shift). Each ellipsoid sits over the part of a particle it describes, at the height of the band it carries. Fitted on the baseline-corrected cube, on the particles only, with every 5th channel held out to measure how well it generalises.
